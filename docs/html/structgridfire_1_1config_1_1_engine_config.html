◆ passiveSpecies

std::vector<std::string> gridfire::config::EngineConfig::passiveSpecies

Species carried in the composition without any reactions (e.g. the metals a stellar code lumps into Z, represented by one isotope): they count towards Y_e and screening, and their abundances never change. A passive species may not take part in any reaction of the network.

◆ useReverseReactions

bool gridfire::config::EngineConfig::useReverseReactions = false

Include reverse (photodisintegration) rates, from detailed balance, for strong reactions with two reactants and two products. Negligible for hydrogen and helium burning, so off by default.
