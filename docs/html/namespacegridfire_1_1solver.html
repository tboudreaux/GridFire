InstantaneousRates gridfire::solver::evaluate_instantaneous_rates ( const engine::DynamicEngine & engine,
engine::scratch::StateBlob & engine_ctx,
const NetIn & netIn,
bool include_channels = true,
bool include_reactions = true )
nodiscard

Instantaneous rates at netIn's state straight from the engine, with no integration.

One calculateRHSAndEnergy call (eps, neutrino losses, dY/dt) and one calculateEpsDerivatives call, plus calculateEnergyChannels when include_channels is set and calculateReactionContributions (each reaction's energy, neutrino loss and neutrino emission rates) when include_reactions is set. netIn.tMax, dt0 and energy are ignored. Network species missing from netIn.composition are taken as absent.

Parameters
engine_ctxScratch state of engine (its own, or one cloned from it); only engine scratch is touched.
Exceptions
exceptions::SolverErrorfor a non-positive or non-finite temperature or density, a negative or non-finite abundance, or a positive abundance of a species that is not in the network.
exceptions::BadRHSEngineErrorif the engine cannot evaluate the RHS.
