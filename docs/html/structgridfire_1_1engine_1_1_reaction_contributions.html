Detailed Description

Structure holding derivatives of energy generation rate with respect to T and rho.

This struct encapsulates the partial derivatives of the specific nuclear energy generation rate with respect to temperature and density.

What each reaction contributes at one state, in getNetworkReactions() order.

The contributions sum to the totals of calculateRHSAndEnergy at the same state: energy_rate to nuclearEnergyGenerationRate, neutrino_energy_loss_rate to neutrinoEnergyLossRate and neutrino_rate to totalNeutrinoFlux. Solar neutrino sources (pp, pep, hep, Be-7, B-8, N-13, O-15, F-17) are individual entries.
